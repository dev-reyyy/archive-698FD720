Hi dear, my love.

If you’re reading this, it means the puzzles are over —
and I don’t need to hide behind the archive anymore.

The whole app was never really about files, codes, or mysteries.
Those were just a long way around
to create something a little special for you.

I wanted you to enjoy the process,
to move through it one step at a time,
and see where it quietly leads.

Now that everything is open,
there’s only one thing left to ask.

***Can I be your date on Valentine’s Day?***

I know it might sound out of nowhere,
but the truth is,
everything here was just a long way
of asking you something that only takes a few words.

***Could I be your Valentine?***
